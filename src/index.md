We're a group of students at the University of Washington interested in competitive programming.

## How do I join?

All UW students are welcome to show up to any of our weekly meetings!
We post time and location on our [Discord]({{ data.discord_link }}).
There's no commitment, registration, or sign-up.

<p><a class="join-discord" href="{{ data.discord_link }}">Join Discord</a></p>

Looking for somewhere to start? Browse our [resource directory](/resources/) for learning guides, practice problems, and tools.

## What do you do?

During meetings, we discuss solutions to problems from our [vjudge group](https://vjudge.net/group/uw-cp-club),
do mini-lectures on algorithms, and play group games.

Each year there's a local programming contest at the University of Washington.
We encourage students to form teams and participate! Learn more in the [ICPC](/icpc) tab.
